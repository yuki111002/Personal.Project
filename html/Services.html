Everything Igbobolo Has to Offer

Swimming Pool

Our pristine pool is fed by natural mountain springs, offering refreshingly cool waters surrounded by lush tropical vegetation. Perfect for relaxation, family fun, or a peaceful morning swim with mountain views.

Natural spring water Mountain view setting Shaded lounging areas

Hiking Adventure

Explore miles of carefully maintained trails that range from gentle nature walks to challenging mountain ascents. Each path reveals unique flora, hidden waterfalls, and panoramic viewpoints.

Multiple difficulties Scenic viewpoints Low Cortisol Hike

Nature Exploration

Beyond the trails, discover the rich biodiversity of our mountain ecosystem. From bird watching to forest meditation spots, every corner of Igbobolo offers a new way to connect with the natural world.

Peaceful ambiance Meditation areas Photography

Outdoor Gatherings

Create lasting memories with outdoor dining, bonfire evenings, and starlit conversations. Our open spaces are perfect for family reunions, celebrations, or simply gathering around a fire.

Bonfire areas Open-air dining Stargazing

Featured Experience

The Pool Oasis

A pristine swimming pool fed by natural mountain springs. Surrounded by lush tropical vegetation and offering panoramic mountain views, it is the perfect place to unwind, cool off, and soak in the beauty of Igbobolo.

Whether you are looking for a refreshing morning swim, a lazy afternoon float, or a magical evening dip under the stars, our pool delivers an experience unlike any other.

Adventure Awaits

Easy Mountain Trail

Lace up your boots and explore miles of carefully maintained trails that wind through ancient forests, past hidden waterfalls, and up to breathtaking viewpoints. Each path offers a unique perspective of Igbobolo's natural beauty.

From gentle nature walks perfect for families to challenging ascents for seasoned hikers, there is a trail for every adventurer at Igbobolo.
